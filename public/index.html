How to read this report

Growth = (September − August) ÷ August. Percentage-point change = September score minus August score. Zero baselines do not have relative growth. Missing values remain unavailable. All quality-target achievement measures are omitted.

Individual averages use paired records, not summed conversion percentages. They are not pooled conversion rates because connected-call denominators are unavailable. PIT floor comparisons use each participant’s own September floor benchmark. Floor benchmarks are not available for the other three activities.

Overview quality uses the reported Grand Total from the configured vertical tab. Filtered vertical results use call-weighted averages; source totals may not reconcile exactly with displayed detail. The separate Q-Score TargetAchievement tab is not blended into these results.

Need Generation retains duplicate CTET LKO entries with source row labels. Activity populations can overlap and are never added into a unique reach total. Rows with incomplete scores remain in detail but are excluded from paired comparisons. PIT source zero quality scores are retained as supplied.

These are observed before/after outcomes following training, not an estimate of causally attributable revenue or ROI. The training approach descriptions were supplied by Rahul Jha. No participation, completion or activity dates are inferred beyond the source.
